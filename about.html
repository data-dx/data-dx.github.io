InfoRec Labs

InfoRec Labs is the personal software project of an independent developer, building practical tools for managing information and digital assets, keeping information and assets always under the user's own control.

Apps

  • InfoRec: a Windows desktop tool for organizing document records and printing forms, truly digitizing your records so they become a resource you can put to use. Learn more
  • IRVault: a non-custodial asset management tool, available as a Solana Mobile app and on the web. View your digital assets in one place by wallet address or exchange API key (app only), send on Solana, BNB Chain and Ethereum, and swap tokens. IRVault never accesses or stores your private keys or seed phrase; every transaction is signed in your own wallet app. Learn more

InfoRec Labs

InfoRec Labs 是獨立開發者的個人軟體開發專案，開發資訊管理與數位資產管理的實用工具，讓資訊與資產始終由使用者自行掌控。

開發的 App

  • InfoRec：Windows 桌面工具，用來整理文件紀錄、套印表單，把資訊紀錄確實數位化，成為使用者可以再運用的資源。了解更多
  • IRVault：Solana Mobile 手機 App 與網頁版，非託管的資產管理工具。可用錢包地址或交易所 API Key（App 版）整合查看虛擬資產，支援 Solana、BNB Chain、Ethereum 的轉帳以及代幣交換。IRVault 不存取、不儲存您的私鑰或助記詞，每筆交易皆在您自己的錢包 App 內簽署。了解更多
