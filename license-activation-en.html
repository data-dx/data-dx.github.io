InfoRec Free Registration Guide

InfoRec registration is free. No payment is required. After downloading, you can use the full trial for 3 months. Print 10 pages during the trial to register free for one year, then print 30 pages each year to renew free of charge.

Registration Requirements

  • Trial: 3 months from the first launch, with all features.
  • Free registration: Print 10 pages during the trial to register for one year.
  • Free renewal: Print 30 pages within one year after registration to register for another year. Renewal opens 30 days before the expiry date, and the new expiry date continues from the current one.
  • How pages are counted: Pages sent to a printer × copies. Print previews do not count, and neither do virtual printers such as PDF, XPS, or OneNote.
  • Check your progress: The "Usage Progress" section of the registration window shows your expiry date, days left, and pages printed. If you have not printed enough pages, the "Submit" button is unavailable and tells you how many pages are left.
  • Not registered or expired: You can keep using InfoRec as the Community Edition, and all existing forms (including official ones) still work. Import Print, online form downloads, and software updates require registration.
  • Network edition (several computers sharing a server database) and CheckIssue: Please email inforec.dev@gmail.com and we will register for you.

Before You Start

  1. Download InfoRec (Windows desktop tool)

    Go to https://www.inforec.dev/download.html, then download and run InfoRec.exe.

  2. Choose how to register
    • Online Registration (recommended): Requires a Solana wallet, such as a Solana wallet browser extension on your computer, or the IRVault app (Solana dApp Store) or another Solana wallet on your phone. The wallet only signs a short message to confirm it is you. Nothing is transferred or charged, and you do not need to hold any tokens.
    • Offline Registration (by Email): For users without a wallet, or computers without internet access. We register for you.
  3. See also: License & Activate, Request License

Option 1: Online Registration (Recommended)

  1. Submit a request in InfoRec
    Open InfoRec.exe, click "Support > License & Activate", then click "Request License". Set "Verification Mode" to "Online Registration", fill in the registered name, contact person, email, and other details, select the hardware to bind (computer name or USB drive), and click "Submit".
  2. Open the registration page
    A guide page opens. To register on this computer, click the button that opens the registration page on this computer. To register on your phone, scan the QR code with the IRVault app or your phone camera.
  3. Connect your wallet and sign
    The registration page shows your usage progress. Once you meet the requirement, connect a Solana wallet and sign the message shown (free of charge). Registration is then complete.
  4. Finish in InfoRec
    Click "Support > License & Activate", then click "Verify" to download the registration result. Restart InfoRec when done.

A wallet registered online can claim IREC credits in the IRVault app via "Claim IREC". IREC records usage and contributions and has nothing to do with payment.

Option 2: Offline Registration (by Email, Registered for You)

For users without a wallet, or when the computer running InfoRec has no internet access.

  1. Create a request file
    Open InfoRec.exe, click "Support > License & Activate", then click "Request License". Set "Verification Mode" to "Offline Registration (by Email)", fill in your details, and click "Submit". InfoRec creates a request file Reg_*.txt and opens its folder.
  2. Send the request file
    Email the file to inforec.dev@gmail.com. After checking your usage progress, we complete the registration and send back a license file OK_Reg_*.txt.
  3. Finish in InfoRec
    • Computer with internet access: click "Support > License & Activate", then click "Verify" to download the registration result.
    • Computer without internet access: select "Authorize from File", click "Verify", and choose the license file you received.
    Restart InfoRec when done.

Notes

  • Hardware binding: Registration is bound to the computer name or a USB drive. If you registered with the computer name, rename your new computer to match the old one when you replace it.
  • Expiry date continues: When you renew, the new expiry date starts from the current one, so renewing early does not lose any remaining days.
  • Earlier paid registrations: Licenses registered under the earlier paid plan remain valid.
  • Help: For questions about registration or using InfoRec, email inforec.dev@gmail.com.
